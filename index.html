02 — Case study

A messaging platform 300 services depend on.

At SS&C Intralinks I architected and own the multi-region event backbone behind 300+ microservices. Three problems define the work:

01 · Change

Evolve contracts without downtime

Led zero-downtime schema migrations across 300+ services, keeping every change backward compatible so producers and consumers never had to deploy in lockstep.

02 · Failure

Make failure boring

Redesigned retry strategies and dead-letter handling so transient faults heal themselves and poison messages are isolated, inspectable and replayable.

03 · Visibility

See problems before users do

Built monitoring and alerting with Prometheus and Grafana around lag, retries and dead letters, which significantly reduced production incidents.
